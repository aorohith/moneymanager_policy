Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the BK-B Smart mobile application (the “App”), related services, and any products offered through the App. The App is operated by Business Standard Private Limited (“BSPL”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). Please read this policy carefully before using the App. By downloading, registering for, or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

When you register or use the App, you may provide information such as:

  • Name, email address, and mobile phone number
  • One-time password (OTP) for verification
  • Profile details (date of birth, gender, address, pin code, locality, state, profile photo, enrollment details)
  • Educational and professional information (courses, education history, skills, resume, job preferences, and related uploads)
  • Content you submit (e.g. quiz responses, case study answers, campus event posts, videos, documents)
  • Communications when you contact support

This information may include “Personal Information” and “Sensitive Personal Information” as defined under the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (collectively, “Information”).

1.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • IP address and general usage data
  • App interaction and navigation data (e.g. screens viewed, features used)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data
  • Push notification tokens (if you enable notifications)
  • Approximate location (when you grant permission, e.g. to assist with pin code or location-related features)

2. Local and Cloud Storage

On your device: The App stores certain data locally (such as login session tokens, user preferences, and cached content) to improve performance and allow offline access where supported.

On our servers: Account, profile, learning activity, job-related data, and content you submit are stored on secure servers operated by us or our service providers, primarily in India.

Local data is cleared when you log out or uninstall the App, subject to technical limitations of your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use Information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features
  • Authenticate you and manage your account
  • Deliver educational content, quizzes, case studies, news, videos, and related services
  • Support job recruitment and profile features where applicable
  • Send push notifications and service-related communications
  • Improve App performance, fix bugs, and ensure security
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used (such as feature usage and engagement). This helps us improve the App experience.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and error logs so we can diagnose and fix technical issues.

We use Firebase Remote Config to deliver configuration updates (such as minimum app version requirements).

Analytics and crash data may be processed by Google as a service provider. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send alerts about quizzes, content, updates, and other App-related activity. You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.

6. Advertising

The App does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs to serve ads. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

7. Device Permissions

The App may request access to:

  • Internet — to connect to our services
  • Camera and photos — for profile pictures, uploads, and video features
  • Storage / files — to save or upload documents and media
  • Location — to support pin code or location-related features (only when permitted)
  • Calendar — to add events such as webinars to your device calendar
  • Notifications — to send push alerts

You can revoke permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work without the required permissions.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share Information:

  • With trusted service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g. hosting, analytics, push delivery)

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

We implement reasonable physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your Information. Access is limited to authorized personnel who need it to operate the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain your Information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type and legal requirements.

11. Your Choices and Rights

You may:
